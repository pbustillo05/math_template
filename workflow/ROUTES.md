# Research routes

Give each materially different approach a stable ID such as `R001`. Link the shortest piece of evidence that explains its current status. Put the selected next check in [CURRENT.md](CURRENT.md) and keep detailed mathematics in the relevant topic notes.

| R001 | Describe the first approach after defining the problem. | `EXPLORATORY` | — | — |

## Route statuses

- `EXPLORATORY` — The approach is being scoped.
- `ACTIVE` — A specific check is underway.
- `PROMISING` — Evidence supports pursuing it and a next check is identifiable.
- `BLOCKED` — A missing step prevents progress. State what would let the route resume.
- `REFUTED` — A counterexample or contradiction defeats a required feature of the approach under its stated assumptions. Link the evidence.
- `CANDIDATE` — A serious argument is assembled and has a specific assessment to undergo.
- `ESTABLISHED` — The route has a complete supported argument. Link its claim and record manuscript review separately in [reviews.json](reviews.json).
