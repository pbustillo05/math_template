# Bounded research cycle

1. **Recover the frontier.** Read [CURRENT.md](CURRENT.md) and the relevant topic README. Open the exact assumptions, source passages, or prior attempts needed for this task. Consult the [route registry](ROUTES.md) when comparing approaches or reopening one.
2. **Choose the question.** Identify one check with a mathematical stopping condition. Examples include proving a lemma under stated hypotheses, finding a counterexample, or identifying the first unsupported implication. When comparing routes, ask what each outcome would change.
3. **Retrieve and investigate.** Use scoped `semantex search` to find likely units, `get` to inspect exact mathematics, and `context` or `deps` for prerequisites. Check any relied-upon source against the retained original. Give independent workstreams separate files, objectives, and stopping conditions.
4. **Record the result.** Save the evidence in the topic notes and update its ledger. Update the route only when the evidence changes its status, blocker, or condition for reopening. A missing step can leave a route `BLOCKED`. A refutation needs evidence that defeats a required part of the strategy.
5. **Hand off.** Update [CURRENT.md](CURRENT.md) with one next question, exact starting inputs, and a stopping condition. Optional milestone entries belong in `notebook/`. Promote complete arguments through the [draft review protocol](../agents/PROTOCOL.md).

The [user manual](../USER-MANUAL.md) includes commands, a worked example, and the notebook build.
