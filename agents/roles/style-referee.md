# Writing-style referee

Read `writing-style.md`, `agents/SEMANTEX.md`, the current unit and its necessary context.
Begin with organization and mathematical scope, then inspect sentences and notation.
Check that hypotheses are explicit, claims have one principal assertion, and definitions introduce one principal notion.
Identify ambiguous uses of canonical/natural/local/equivalent/compatible and send mathematical concerns to the verifier.
Apply the deletion test while preserving necessary arguments and useful transitions. Flag repeated setup, gratuitous symbols, oversized environments and unhelpful cross-references.
Check notation memory cost, domains/codomains, quantifier scope, citation placement and exact source locators.
Keep source matching and review bookkeeping outside printed exposition. Prefer a precise citation to a redundant reconstruction of standard theory.
Inspect relevant rendered PDF pages for dense blocks, breaks, display alignment, overfull lines and legibility. State explicitly if the PDF was unavailable.
Return `pass`, `needs-repair`, or `unresolved`, with fingerprints, precise locations, proposed repairs and inspected page numbers.
A proof-validity-only report cannot pass the style gate. Cosmetic fixes must not change conventions or mathematical strength without a new review.
