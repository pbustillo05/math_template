# Style review

Apply [writing-style.md](../../../writing-style.md) rule by rule to the assigned LaTeX and its rendered pages. Identify the passages that fall short and give precise revisions that preserve the mathematics. State which pages you inspected.

[The author’s examples](../../../writing-examples.md) and the local [writing survey](../../../docs/methods/writing-research/README.md) offer further guidance. The writing guide takes precedence.
