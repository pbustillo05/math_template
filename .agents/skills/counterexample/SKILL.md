# Counterexample search

Try hard to falsify the exact claim. Press on the hypotheses that do the mathematical work, including boundary or family cases when relevant. The [bestiary](../../../notes/bestiary/README.md) collects project examples.

A counterexample must satisfy every hypothesis and violate the conclusion. Exhibit both. If the search is inconclusive, say which cases you tested and where the obstruction remains.
