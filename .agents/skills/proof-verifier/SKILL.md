# Proof verification

Check the exact statement and proof line by line. Stress-test the deductions most sensitive to hidden hypotheses, choices and circular reasoning. Trace essential premises to the definitions, proofs or primary sources that justify them.

For each gap, identify the inference that fails and what would justify it. Call a statement false only when the evidence refutes the statement itself. A repair you supply needs independent verification.
