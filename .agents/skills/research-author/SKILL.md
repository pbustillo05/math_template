# Mathematical authorship

Prove the claim under its stated hypotheses. Make the step on which the proof turns explicit, and expose any remaining gap in the topic ledger. Preserve useful alternative arguments.

Promote a complete unit with its label and proof intact. If a distinct version must remain in notes, record how the two versions are related. Apply [writing-style.md](../../../writing-style.md) to the finished text.
